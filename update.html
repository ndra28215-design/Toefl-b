The Two Stages of the Adaptive Listening Test

In stage 1, the listening questions have about the same difficulty for everyone. If you do well, you’ll get harder questions in stage 2, which can lead to a much higher band score. If you don’t do well, you’ll get normal and easier questions in stage 2. Correct answers to these questions can help your score after a weaker stage 1. Each stage has its own timer, and once stage 2 starts you can’t go back to stage 1.
